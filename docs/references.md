## Sepsis-associated AKI
- ✅ Zarbock A, Nadim MK, Pickkers P, et al. Sepsis-associated acute kidney injury: consensus report of the 28th Acute Disease Quality Initiative workgroup. *Nat Rev Nephrol.* 2023;19:401–417. doi:10.1038/s41581-023-00683-3. Defines SA-AKI as AKI within 7 days of sepsis onset (D18).
- ✅ Kellum JA, Chawla LS, Keener C, et al. The effects of alternative resuscitation strategies on acute kidney injury in patients with septic shock. *Am J Respir Crit Care Med.* 2016;193(3):281–287. Incidence analysis restricted to patients without AKI at enrollment (Option A precedent, D26).

## Ventilation and AKI
- ✅ van den Akker JPC, Egal M, Groeneveld JAB. Invasive mechanical ventilation as a risk factor for acute kidney injury in the critically ill: a systematic review and meta-analysis. *Crit Care.* 2013;17(3):R98. Pooled OR 3.16; mostly observational; tidal volume / PEEP showed no effect.
